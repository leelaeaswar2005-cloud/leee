The Art of Teaching,
The Science of Music.

For more than a quarter-century, Aura has nurtured artists in rooms made for listening. Our studio doors are open to every age and ambition, with individual attention, remarkable instruments, and an uncompromising love for the craft.

From the first carefully held chord to a final bow beneath the lights, we teach the whole musician: confidence, curiosity, technique, and expression.

Head of Guitar & Composition

With over 10 years on concert stages and in teaching studios, V Prasanna Brings an encouraging, friendly energy to every guitar and vocal lesson. Drawing on years of experience performing in sacred TTD devotional concerts and diverse live bands, he transforms intricate techniques into simple, practical skills that any student can immediately understand and apply..
